Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026 · Effective: March 1, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of the products, services, and platforms provided by Nakama AI Private Limited ("Nakama AI", "we", "us", or "our"). This AUP is incorporated into and forms part of our Terms of Service.

By using our Services, you agree to comply with this AUP. Violations may result in suspension or termination of your account and access to the Services.

1. General Conduct

You agree to use our Services responsibly and in accordance with all applicable laws and regulations. You will not use the Services in any manner that could damage, disable, overburden, or impair the Services or interfere with other users' ability to use the Services.

2. Prohibited Content

You may not use the Services to create, upload, store, distribute, or transmit any content that:

  • Is illegal or promotes illegal activities in any jurisdiction.
  • Depicts, promotes, or facilitates child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or the exploitation of minors.
  • Contains or promotes extreme violence, gore, or torture.
  • Promotes terrorism, violent extremism, or incites violence against individuals or groups.
  • Contains defamatory, libelous, or knowingly false statements about individuals or organizations.
  • Constitutes harassment, bullying, or threats against any person.
  • Promotes discrimination or hatred based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics.
  • Contains non-consensual intimate imagery or "revenge porn."
  • Infringes on intellectual property rights, including copyrights, trademarks, or trade secrets.
  • Contains malware, phishing content, or other malicious code.

3. Prohibited Activities

You may not engage in the following activities when using the Services:

  • Unauthorized Access: Attempting to access accounts, systems, or data that you are not authorized to access.
  • Reverse Engineering: Attempting to reverse-engineer, decompile, disassemble, or extract the source code, underlying algorithms, or AI models of the Services.
  • Competitive Use: Using the Services to develop a competing product or service, or to benchmark the Services for competitive purposes without our written consent.
  • Rate Limit Circumvention: Bypassing or attempting to bypass usage limits, rate limits, or other access controls.
  • Automated Abuse: Using bots, scrapers, or automated tools to access the Services in a manner that exceeds normal human usage patterns or violates our API terms.
  • Impersonation: Impersonating any person, entity, or organization, or misrepresenting your identity or affiliation.
  • Fraud: Using the Services for fraudulent purposes, including creating deceptive content intended to mislead consumers or manipulate financial markets.
  • Deepfakes Without Disclosure: Creating realistic synthetic media of real individuals without clear disclosure that the content is AI-generated.
  • Spam: Using the Services to generate or distribute unsolicited bulk communications.
  • Resource Abuse: Deliberately consuming excessive resources in a manner that degrades the experience for other users.

4. Industry-Specific Guidelines

4.1 Healthcare

The Services are not intended to provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. AI agents deployed in healthcare settings should be used as assistive tools under human supervision, not as replacements for qualified medical professionals.

4.2 Financial Services

AI agents deployed in financial services must comply with all applicable financial regulations. The Services should not be used to provide personalized financial advice without appropriate licensing and disclosures.

4.3 Legal Services

AI-generated content should not be represented as legal advice. Users deploying AI agents for legal-related tasks must ensure compliance with applicable regulations regarding the practice of law.

5. Content Moderation

We employ automated and manual content moderation systems to detect and prevent violations of this AUP. We reserve the right to:

  • Remove or restrict access to any content that violates this AUP.
  • Suspend or terminate accounts that violate this AUP.
  • Report illegal content or activities to appropriate law enforcement authorities.
  • Cooperate with law enforcement investigations as required by law.

6. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of any violation of this AUP, please report it to us immediately at abuse@nakama-ai.com. Please include as much detail as possible, including URLs, screenshots, and a description of the violation. We will investigate all reports promptly and take appropriate action.

7. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this AUP may result in:

  • Warning notification and request to cease the prohibited activity.
  • Temporary suspension of access to the Services.
  • Permanent termination of your account without refund.
  • Legal action where appropriate.
  • Reporting to law enforcement authorities.

The severity of the response will be proportional to the nature and severity of the violation.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this AUP from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated to registered users via email.
