Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026 · Effective: March 1, 2026

This Refund and Cancellation Policy ("Policy") applies to all paid subscriptions and purchases made through the Services provided by Nakama AI Private Limited ("Nakama AI", "we", "us", or "our"). This Policy is incorporated into and forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Subscription Plans

1.1 Monthly Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions are billed at the beginning of each billing cycle. You may cancel a monthly subscription at any time. Upon cancellation:

  • Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.
  • You will not be charged for subsequent billing periods.
  • No prorated refund will be issued for the remaining days in the current billing period.

1.2 Annual Subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are billed as a single payment at the beginning of the subscription term. Annual plans benefit from a discounted rate compared to monthly billing.

  • You may cancel an annual subscription at any time. Your access will continue until the end of the annual billing period.
  • A 30-day money-back guarantee applies to annual subscriptions. If you cancel within 30 days of your initial purchase or renewal, you are eligible for a full refund.
  • After 30 days, no refund will be issued for the remainder of the annual term.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be issued in the following circumstances:

  • 30-Day Guarantee (Annual Plans): Full refund if cancellation is requested within 30 calendar days of the initial purchase or renewal date.
  • Service Outage: If the Services experience downtime exceeding the guaranteed SLA (99.9% uptime) in a given billing period, you may be eligible for service credits or a partial refund proportional to the downtime experienced.
  • Billing Errors: If you are charged incorrectly (e.g., duplicate charges, wrong plan amount), we will issue a full refund for the erroneous charge.
  • Unauthorized Charges: If your payment method was used without your authorization, please contact us immediately and we will investigate and refund confirmed unauthorized charges.

3. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Monthly subscription fees after the billing cycle has begun.
  • Annual subscription fees after the 30-day money-back guarantee period has elapsed.
  • Usage-based charges (e.g., API calls, additional image generations) that have already been consumed.
  • Enterprise service fees covered under a separate Enterprise Service Agreement (refund terms in such agreements supersede this Policy).
  • Any amounts discounted through promotional offers, coupons, or credits.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  1. Email us at billing@nakama-ai.com with the subject line "Refund Request".
  2. Include your full name, registered email address, subscription plan, and the reason for your refund request.
  3. Our billing team will acknowledge your request within 2 business days.
  4. Approved refunds will be processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method.

Please note that it may take an additional 5–10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or payment provider.

5. How to Cancel Your Subscription

You may cancel your subscription at any time by:

  • Logging into your account and navigating to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription.
  • Emailing support@nakama-ai.com with the subject "Cancellation Request".

Upon cancellation, you will retain access to the Services until the end of your current billing period. After the billing period ends, your account will be downgraded to the free tier (if available) or deactivated.

6. Downgrade and Plan Changes

You may downgrade your subscription to a lower-tier plan at any time. Downgrades take effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle. No refund is issued for the difference between plan levels during the current billing period. Any features exclusive to the higher-tier plan will become unavailable upon downgrade.

7. Free Tier and Trial Periods

Free tier accounts and trial periods are provided at no cost and are not subject to refunds. If you choose not to upgrade after a trial period, your account will automatically revert to the free tier with limited features.

8. Dispute Resolution

If you are dissatisfied with the outcome of a refund request, you may escalate the matter by contacting our support team at support@nakama-ai.com or writing to us at the address below. We will make every reasonable effort to resolve your concern within 15 business days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated via email. Continued use of the Services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
