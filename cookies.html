Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026 · Effective: March 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Nakama AI Private Limited ("Nakama AI", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our websites or use our Services. This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and supply information to website operators. Cookies can be "persistent" (stored until they expire or are deleted) or "session" (deleted when you close your browser).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of our website and Services. They enable core functionality such as security, authentication, and session management. You cannot opt out of these cookies as the Services would not function properly without them.

  • Session ID: Maintains your session while you use the Services. Expires when you close your browser.
  • Authentication Token: Keeps you logged in securely. Expires after 30 days or when you log out.
  • CSRF Token: Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks. Session cookie.
  • Cookie Consent: Remembers your cookie preferences. Expires after 12 months.

2.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. They help us improve our website and Services.

  • Google Analytics (_ga, _gid): Collects anonymous usage statistics including pages visited, time on site, and referral sources. Expires after 2 years (_ga) or 24 hours (_gid).
  • Amplitude: Tracks product usage patterns to help us improve features and user experience. Expires after 12 months.

2.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences and settings.

  • Language Preference: Remembers your preferred language. Expires after 12 months.
  • Theme Preference: Remembers your display preferences (e.g., dark mode). Expires after 12 months.
  • Recently Used: Stores your recent activity for quick access. Expires after 30 days.

2.4 Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to track visitors across websites and display ads that are relevant and engaging. We only use these with your explicit consent.

  • Google Ads (conversion tracking): Measures the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. Expires after 90 days.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag: Enables LinkedIn ad targeting and conversion tracking. Expires after 6 months.

3. Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use the following technologies:

  • Web Beacons (Pixel Tags): Small transparent images embedded in emails or web pages to track whether content has been viewed.
  • Local Storage: Used to store data locally on your device for performance optimization and user preferences.
  • Session Storage: Similar to local storage but cleared when you close your browser tab.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You have several options for managing cookies:

4.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in our website footer.

4.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View cookies stored on your device.
  • Delete individual or all cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific or all websites.
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being set.

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of our Services.

4.3 Opt-Out Links

  • Google Analytics: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Google Ads: Manage ad preferences at Google Ad Settings.
  • General: Visit YourAdChoices or Your Online Choices (EU).

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is currently no industry-wide standard for how companies should respond to DNT signals. We do not currently respond to DNT signals, but we respect your cookie preferences as set through our consent banner.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our business practices. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
